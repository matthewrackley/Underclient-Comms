Privacy Policy for Underclient Comms

Last updated: May 21, 2026

1. Introduction

Underclient Comms (the “App” or “Service”) is an embedded chat application that runs inside Discord and is operated by Matthew Rackley through the project name Marack.dev (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect from you when you use Underclient Comms, how we use it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have.

Our Terms of Service, available separately, governs your use of the App. By using the App you agree to both the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy. If you have questions or requests regarding privacy, contact us at support@pornova18.com.

2. Age restriction and children

Underclient Comms is intended only for people aged 18 years or older. We do not knowingly allow or solicit anyone under 18 to use the App or knowingly collect personal information from them. If we learn that someone under 18 is using the App, we will suspend access and delete associated data. If you believe a minor has used the App, please contact us immediately.

3. Information we collect

We collect three broad categories of information:

3.1 Information from Discord authentication

To use the App you must authenticate via Discord. Depending on the permissions and scopes granted through Discord’s OAuth/SDK, we may receive:

  • your Discord user ID, username, display name or global name;
  • avatar information;
  • IDs of the server, channel or voice channel you are connected to;
  • authentication tokens and expiration times; and
  • other data that Discord returns through its authentication flow.

Discord controls its own collection and use of data, which is governed by Discord’s own policies. We use the Discord data only to operate Underclient Comms and do not receive your password or payment information from Discord.

3.2 Information you provide through the App

You may provide data directly when you use the App. This includes:

  • chat messages you send;
  • room names, access codes and settings (once private rooms are enabled);
  • profile information and user settings (once these features are enabled);
  • uploaded images, videos, files or links (if uploads are introduced);
  • moderation reports or support messages.

3.3 Metadata and technical data

For each message or room we may store metadata such as the message ID, sender Discord ID, server and channel IDs, timestamps, room IDs and expiration timestamps. We do not intentionally log IP addresses or create device or browser fingerprints as part of normal chat operations, but technical logs from hosting providers or security tools may capture such data temporarily. If additional technical data collection becomes necessary for security, abuse prevention or analytics, we will update this policy.

Payment and monetization data (such as subscription status, transaction IDs or donation details) is not currently collected because the App is free. If we later add paid features, we will use third‑party payment processors to handle financial data; those providers may collect additional information under their own policies.

4. How we use information

We use the information described above to operate, secure and improve Underclient Comms, and to comply with legal and contractual obligations. In particular, we use data to:

  • authenticate you and connect you to the right server, channel or private room;
  • display and transmit chat messages between participants;
  • store messages temporarily so users can read conversations;
  • maintain and enforce retention periods (seven days for general chat and twenty‑four hours for private rooms once enabled);
  • respond to support enquiries and bug reports;
  • troubleshoot errors, detect abuse, enforce our Terms of Service and legal obligations;
  • improve the App, develop new features and, if enabled in future, support paid features or analytics;
  • protect the security and integrity of our systems and users.

5. Legal bases for processing

Where required by law (for example under the EU’s General Data Protection Regulation), we rely on one or more legal bases to process personal data: (a) Contract, because processing is necessary to provide and enforce the Terms of Service; (b) Consent, where you voluntarily submit data or grant certain permissions; (c) Legitimate interests, such as operating, securing and improving the App; and (d) Legal obligation, where we are required to comply with applicable laws or respond to lawful requests. In the context of Firebase, Google acts as a processor for customer data while we, as the App operator, are the controller【245562276903159†L430-L443】.

6. Sharing and disclosure of information

We share information only in the circumstances described below:

6.1 With other users

Messages you send are visible to other participants in the same server, channel or private room. Private rooms (once enabled) restrict access but do not guarantee confidentiality; anyone with access may copy or reshare content.

6.2 With Discord

Because the App runs on Discord, Discord processes your account and usage data under its own policies. We do not control Discord’s use of data.

6.3 With service providers

We use Firebase/Firestore and related Google services to store and process App data. Under data‑protection law, Google is a processor for customer data, and customers (like us) act as controllers【245562276903159†L430-L443】. We may also use hosting providers, security services, support tools, analytics providers or payment processors (once monetization is added). These service providers may access information only as needed to perform services on our behalf, and are required to protect the data.

6.4 With server owners and moderators

Today the App does not provide formal moderation tools. In future, we may allow server owners or authorized moderators to manage Underclient Comms usage within their server. That may involve giving moderators access to specific messages, reports or user IDs relevant to their server. Moderators will be bound by Discord’s rules, our Terms of Service and this policy.

6.5 For legal or safety reasons

We may disclose information if we believe in good faith that doing so is required by law; to respond to subpoenas, court orders or other legal requests; to cooperate with law enforcement or regulators; to enforce our Terms of Service; to protect users from harm; or to investigate and prevent fraud, abuse or illegal activity.

6.6 Business transfers

If we participate in a merger, acquisition, restructuring or sale of assets, user information may be transferred to the acquiring entity and will remain subject to this policy unless otherwise stated.

7. Cookies and local storage

Underclient Comms may use browser local storage or similar technologies to maintain your session, remember settings and keep the App working properly. Disabling these technologies in your browser may cause the App to malfunction. Discord and our service providers may also use cookies or local storage; their policies apply to those uses.

8. Security measures

We employ reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data against unauthorized access, loss or alteration. These include server‑side security rules, access controls, token management, encryption in transit, and regular monitoring. Nevertheless, no system can guarantee perfect security. You use the App at your own risk and should not share anything that you require to remain secret or confidential.

9. Data retention

We retain data only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. Currently:

  • General chat messages are retained for up to seven (7) days, after which they are scheduled for deletion.
  • Private‑room messages (once enabled) are retained for up to twenty‑four (24) hours.
  • Support tickets and moderation records (once these features are enabled) may be retained as long as needed for legal, safety or business reasons.
  • Technical logs may be retained briefly for debugging and security.

Deleted or expired data may not disappear immediately from backups or caches. We cannot guarantee that all deleted messages will be permanently removed from every backup or system copy, but we will endeavour to honour the retention periods.
